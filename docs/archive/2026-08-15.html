Hypothetical Classification: Let the LLM Hallucinate Tags, Then Match—a Better Tagging Pattern

Doug Turnbull's technique inverts the standard classification prompt: instead of asking the model to pick from a huge list of existing tags (which fails at scale), ask it to freely generate tags for the content, then match those hallucinated tags against your taxonomy. It's cheaper, scales to large tag sets, and outperforms brute-force classification.

Why it matters: Any product with user-generated content and a large taxonomy—e-commerce catalogs, knowledge bases, support tickets—can apply this pattern to cut classification costs and improve accuracy.
