Generative AI consumes significantly more energy processing non-English text due to tokenizer inefficiency

Research shows that because major LLM tokenizers were trained predominantly on English, non-English inputs require more tokens to encode the same semantic content, directly increasing compute and energy cost per query. For languages like Arabic or Thai, the cost per unit of meaning can be 3–10x higher than English.

Why it matters: If you're building or pricing AI products for non-English markets, your cost model and latency assumptions are likely wrong — and this creates a structural moat for teams who build multilingual-efficient inference pipelines.
